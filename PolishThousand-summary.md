# PolishThousand Summary

## Idea

`PolishThousand` is a simple mobile app for Russian- and Ukrainian-speaking users who need basic Polish for everyday life in Poland.

The core promise is:

`Learn the first 1000 useful Polish words for life, work, and daily communication in Poland.`

## Product Direction

- Keep the app very simple and clear.
- Focus on one practical outcome, not a full language course.
- Build around useful vocabulary in real-life context.
- Show visible progress and a feeling of completion.

## Target Audience

- Russian-speaking users living in or moving to Poland
- Ukrainian-speaking users living in or moving to Poland
- People who need practical Polish fast: shopping, transport, work, documents, doctor, housing

## MVP

Start with `250-300` words, not `1000`.

Topics for MVP:

- Basic phrases
- Numbers, dates, time
- Shop and groceries
- Transport
- Housing and rent
- Work
- Documents and services
- Doctor and pharmacy
- Daily communication
- Food and cafe

## Learning Unit

Each word should include:

- Polish word
- Russian translation
- Ukrainian translation
- audio for the word
- one short real-life sentence
- translation of the sentence
- audio for the sentence

Example:

- `sklep`
- `магазин`
- `магазин`
- sentence: `Idę do sklepu.`

## Exercises

Keep only a few exercise types in MVP:

- choose translation
- listen and choose
- understand the sentence in context

## UX Principles

- no overload
- no account required in MVP
- offline-first
- 5-10 minute sessions
- clear progress screen
- simple achievements / ladder / milestones

## Monetization

For the first version, prefer a `one-time unlock` instead of subscription.

Suggested model:

- free: first 2-3 topics
- paid: unlock full course

## Technical Direction

- KMP / shared logic for Android + iOS
- static content stored as `JSON`
- audio files stored separately in app resources
- user progress stored locally
- no backend in MVP

## Immediate Goal

The goal is not a big edtech platform.

The goal is:

- ship a small real app
- publish to Google Play and App Store
- validate that users understand the offer
- reach first small revenue, for example `$100/month`

## Current Product Positioning

`Polish 1000`

Positioning:

`First 1000 Polish words for life in Poland`
